# Project Overview – Identity Governance & Regulatory Compliance

## Objective
Implement identity governance controls in Microsoft Entra ID to enforce least privilege, support regulatory compliance, and reduce identity-related risks.

## Business Problem
The organization lacks structured access governance, resulting in:
- Excessive permissions
- No periodic access reviews
- Increased audit and compliance risk

## Scope
Included:
- Access Reviews
- Privileged Identity Management (PIM)
- Audit logging and evidence collection

## Success Criteria
- Periodic access reviews enforced
- Privileged access is time-bound and approved
- Audit-ready evidence is available
